# Importing your Instagram archive

With the *Import Instagram* permission, granted by an administrator, you bring back your whole Instagram history
(*Réglages › Instagram › Importer mon archive*, the settings): posts, carousels, videos, archived posts and stories,
with their original dates. An import in progress is shown there, with a button to resume it.

## Requesting the archive from Instagram

In Instagram: *Accounts Center › Your information and permissions › Download your information*. Choose the **JSON**
format, from the beginning, and at least *Content* (posts, stories). Instagram sends one or more `.zip` files.

## Uploading the archive

Upload the `.zip` files (up to 20 GB in total). The upload is sent in chunks and **resumes** after an interruption.
Private messages are never read.

## Choosing what is imported

Once the archive is analysed, choose the visibility of the import, then, post by post, another visibility,
*Archiver* (archive) or *Ne pas importer* (skip). A default audience group is optional.

## After the import

Posts keep their date, carousels their order; posts archived on Instagram go to your archive and stories to your story
archive. Running an import again creates no duplicates.

Imported public posts are not announced to your fediverse followers, so as not to flood them. In imported captions,
`@name` leads to that account's Instagram profile.
