# Notifications

## In the app

*Notifications* lists what concerns you, from here and from the fediverse: likes, comments, mentions and follow
requests. A notification opens the related post or page and is marked as read; it can also be marked as unread or
deleted, one by one or all at once.

Administrators are also told about new accounts: the notification leads to the administration, to grant the account
its rights, or to the profile of a Mastodon account signing in for the first time.

## On this device

*Notifications › Sur cet appareil* turns on notifications on the phone or computer (on iPhone and iPad, after
[installing the app](premiers-pas.md#installing-the-app)). Each type can be switched off:

- new posts and new stories of the accounts you follow;
- likes and comments on your posts;
- follow requests to approve.

The choice applies to every enabled device; *Désactiver* stops them on this one.
