# Posting

The **Créer** button offers *Publication* (post), *Texte* (text) or *Story* (see [Stories](stories.md)). It requires the
*Publication* permission, granted by an administrator.

## Creating a post

- Up to **20 photos and videos**, from the device or a connected photo library ([Immich or Synology
  Photos](phototheques.md)). Selections add up; reorder by drag and drop or with ◀ ▶; the magnifier opens a large
  preview; remove a media from the preview.
- **Alt text** for each media: describe the image for people using a screen reader.
- **Caption**, with `#hashtags`, mentions and links (see [Mentions, hashtags and links](mentions-et-liens.md)).
- Optional **location**.
- Optional **content warning**: media stay blurred until someone chooses to see them.

Originals and their metadata are kept; public copies do not contain your GPS position.

## Posting a text

At the top of *Nouvelle publication*, two tabs: **Photos et vidéos** and **Texte**. The Texte tab posts a text alone,
without photo or video, up to 2,200 characters, with `#hashtags`, mentions and links. It puts the text forward and
hides what only media need: music and posting to Instagram. The text already written is kept when switching tabs.

When the text contains a link, the post shows a **preview** of the first one: site, title, description and image of
the page. The image is copied to your instance: visitors load nothing from the linked site.

A text post goes to the fediverse as an ordinary message, readable from Mastodon and other networks.

## Adding music

*Ajouter une musique* searches Creative Commons tracks on Openverse: preview, then choose where the 30-second loop
starts. The track plays while the post is on screen; its credits (title, artist, license) show under the author. Your
favorites come first.

## Visibility

| Visibility | Who sees it | Sent to the fediverse |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Public* | Everyone, in public feeds | Yes |
| *Non répertorié* (unlisted) | Anyone with the link, not in feeds | No |
| *Abonnés* (followers) | Your accepted followers | Yes |
| *Groupes* (groups) | Followers who belong to the chosen groups (see [Audience groups](groupes.md)) | No |
| *Privé* (private) | Only you | No |

## After posting

Photos are converted to WebP and videos to MP4 in the background. A post with a video stays *en préparation*
(processing) during the conversion, with its progress on your profile; it then appears by itself.

## Editing, archiving or deleting

From the **⋯** menu of a post:

- **Modifier** (edit): caption, location, visibility, content warning and media;
- **Archiver** (archive): the post leaves your profile and the feeds but stays visible to you in *Archives*; unarchive
  it to bring it back;
- **Supprimer** (delete): permanently, here and in the fediverse.
