# Instagram

## Also posting to Instagram

*Réglages › Instagram* connects a **professional** Instagram account (Creator or Business, required by Instagram).
Rules choose for which visibilities *Publier aussi sur Instagram* is ticked in advance, and whether they apply to
stories; the box can still be changed for each post.

Sending happens in the background once the media are ready. Its status shows on the post, with *Réessayer* (retry) if
it failed. The connection needs a Meta app, which the instance administrator may provide.

A post already online and never sent can go afterwards with **Publier sur Instagram** in the **⋯** menu (feed,
profile or post page), or by ticking *Publier aussi sur Instagram* in *Modifier* (edit). If a video was just added,
sending waits until it is ready. Text posts are not sent: Instagram only accepts photos and videos.

## Sharing to Instagram

Without a professional account, **Partager vers Instagram** (**⋯** menu of a post, or *Mes stories*) opens the phone's
share sheet with the photos and videos and copies the caption: paste it in Instagram. On a computer, the files are
downloaded.

## Mentioning an Instagram account

`ig:@name` in a caption becomes a link to that Instagram account, and a real mention in the copy sent to Instagram
(see [Mentions, hashtags and links](mentions-et-liens.md)).
