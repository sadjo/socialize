# With a Mastodon account

If the instance allows it, someone who already has a Mastodon account uses Socialize **without creating an account
here**, from the *Mastodon* tab of the sign-in screen.

## Signing in with Mastodon

1. Choose your **server** (for example mastodon.social) and enter your **username**, without the @: `alice` for
   `@alice@mastodon.social`. A full address `@alice@server` works too.
2. *Continuer sur Mastodon* opens your server's page: type your password there, then authorize Socialize.
3. Back here, you are signed in.

The links below the form lead to the chosen server: *Créer un compte Mastodon* and *Mot de passe Mastodon oublié ?*.

## What changes with a Mastodon account

- **Profile**: it shows your Mastodon posts; the profile is edited on your Mastodon server.
- **Feed**: posts of the accounts you follow that the instance knows, local accounts included.
- **Stories**: those of the local accounts you follow.
- **Likes, comments and follows** go through your Mastodon server.
- **Followers**: if your Mastodon account approves its followers itself, requests coming from here are accepted or
  refused in *Demandes*, and a follower is removed from your followers list; your Mastodon server takes care of it.
- **Notifications**: likes, comments, mentions and follow requests coming from here show up in *Notifications*, as for
  a local account; your Mastodon app notifies you as well. Device notifications remain those of your Mastodon app.
- **Appearance**: theme and media filter are chosen here, in *Apparence* (menu); the other settings stay on your
  Mastodon server.
- **Posting**: photos, videos and texts posted from Socialize go to your Mastodon account; the *Texte* tab posts a
  message without media.
- **Instagram import**: possible if an administrator allows it; the archive is posted to your Mastodon account.

Socialize acts with the authorization granted when signing in, never knowing your Mastodon password.

## Creating a Mastodon account

If the administrator set it up, the sign-in screen also offers to create an account on a chosen Mastodon server.
