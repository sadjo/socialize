# Following and followers

## Finding accounts

*Rechercher* (search) finds accounts by name, or by fediverse address (`@name@instance`). Following a remote account
imports its recent photo and video posts.

## Following an account

*Suivre* (follow) on its profile. If the account approves its followers, the button shows *Demande envoyée* (request
sent) until it agrees; one click cancels the request. Its followers-only posts become visible once the request is
accepted.

## Follow requests

With the *Valider moi-même les demandes d'abonnement* setting (see [Privacy](compte-et-reglages.md#privacy)), every
request is notified. The *Demandes* (requests) page, opened from your profile counter or the notification, lists the
pending accounts with **Accepter** (accept) and **Refuser** (refuse). A refused account is not explicitly told; it can
ask again later.

## Managing followers and followed accounts

On your profile, the *abonnés* (followers) and *abonnements* (following) counters open the lists: **Retirer** (remove)
a follower (they are not told) or **Ne plus suivre** (unfollow) an account.
