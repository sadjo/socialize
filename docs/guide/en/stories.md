# Stories

A story is a photo or a video visible for **24 hours**.

## Posting a story

*Créer › Story*: up to 10 photos or videos at once, each one becoming a story. Overlay text and music are optional.
Visibility is chosen as for a post: everyone, your followers or [groups](groupes.md).

## Watching stories

The stories bar at the top of the feed shows yours first, then the unseen stories of the accounts you follow. Tap on
the right to go to the next one, on the left to go back; press and hold to pause.

You can see who viewed your stories.

## Featuring a story

An expired story moves to your story archive. From *Mes stories*, **feature** it (*à la une*): it then stays on your
profile, under the profile picture, grouped with others under a title of your choice.
