# Feed and interactions

## The feeds

- **Publications** (home): your posts and those of the accounts you follow, newest first;
- **Local**: public posts of the accounts of this instance;
- **Fédéré** (federated): public posts received from the fediverse, from accounts followed by people here.

## Likes and comments

- **Like** with the heart or a double tap on the media; the counter shows who liked.
- **Comments** right in the feed or on the post page. The author of a comment, like the author of the post, can delete
  it.

## The post menu

The **⋯** menu opens the post, shares it (link, email, Facebook, Instagram, system share sheet), shows it on its
original instance, and for your own posts edits, archives or deletes it.

## Photos and videos

Videos play muted when on screen; the sound button applies to all of them. Clicking a photo or a video opens the
full-screen viewer: arrows, keyboard or swipe to move between media.
