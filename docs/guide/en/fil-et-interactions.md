# Feed and interactions

## The feeds

- **Publications** (home): your posts and those of the accounts you follow, newest first, in three tabs:
  - **Abonnements** (following): all the accounts you follow;
  - **Local**: the accounts of this instance you follow, and your own posts;
  - **Fédéré** (federated): the fediverse accounts you follow (Mastodon, Pixelfed…);
- **Local** (menu): all public posts of the accounts of this instance, followed or not, to discover new ones.

## Text posts

Posts without photo or video, from here or from the fediverse, show in the feed like the others: the text in place of
the media, cut after six lines (*Afficher plus* expands it), with a preview of their first link. With a content
warning, the text waits until someone chooses to show it.

On a profile, two tabs: **Photos**, the grid of posts with media, and **Textes**, the list of text posts, each with
its count. The profile opens on the one with more posts.

The profile of a fediverse account shows its recent posts, even if nobody follows it here yet, and the follower,
following and post counts given by its instance.

## Likes and comments

- **Like** with the heart or a double tap on the media; the counter shows who liked.
- **Comments** right in the feed or on the post page. The author of a comment, like the author of the post, can delete
  it.

## The post menu

The **⋯** menu opens the post, shares it (link, email, Facebook, Instagram, system share sheet), shows it on its
original instance, and for your own posts edits, archives or deletes it.

## Photos and videos

Videos play muted when on screen; the sound button applies to all of them. Clicking a photo or a video opens the
full-screen viewer: arrows, keyboard or swipe to move between media.
