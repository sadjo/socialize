# Audience groups

Groups restrict posts and stories to some of your followers: family, close friends…

## Creating a group

*Réglages › Groupes*: create a group, name it and pick its members among your followers. A follower can belong to
several groups, and Mastodon accounts signed in here can be members.

## Posting to a group

When posting, choose the *Groupes* visibility and one or more groups. Members see a *Groupe* badge on the post; only
you see the group names.

Group posts stay on the instance: they are never sent to the fediverse.

Deleting a group makes the posts that only targeted it private.
