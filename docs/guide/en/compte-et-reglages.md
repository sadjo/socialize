# Account and settings

Everything is set in *Réglages* (settings), tab by tab.

## Profile

*Réglages › Profil*: display name, biography, profile picture and cover image (4:1 banner). Both images are cropped in
the browser (drag, zoom) and can be repositioned later without uploading them again. The biography accepts mentions
and links.

## Privacy

Two checkboxes in *Réglages › Profil*:

- **Réserver mes listes à mes abonnés**: other visitors only see how many followers and followed accounts you have, not
  the lists.
- **Valider moi-même les demandes d'abonnement**: every new follower, local or from the fediverse, waits for your
  approval before seeing your followers-only posts (see [Follow requests](suivre.md#follow-requests)). Unticking it
  accepts the pending requests.

## Password

*Réglages › Mot de passe*: enter your current password, then the new one twice (at least 10 characters; a password
known to have leaked is refused). Your other devices and browsers are signed out, this one stays signed in.

Forgot your password: ask an administrator to choose a new one for you.

## Appearance

*Réglages › Apparence*, personal and applied on all your devices:

- **Theme**: light, dark, night violet or automatic (follows the device setting), previewed instantly;
- **Media filter**: off, light, medium or strong. It softens the colors of photos and videos in feeds and grids; post
  pages, the viewer and stories keep the original colors. Your files are never modified.

## Other tabs

- *Groupes*: your [audience groups](groupes.md);
- *Immich* and *Synology*: your [photo libraries](phototheques.md);
- *Instagram*: [posting to Instagram](instagram.md#also-posting-to-instagram).
