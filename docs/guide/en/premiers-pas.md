# Getting started

Socialize is a photo and video network: you post photos, videos and stories, follow accounts and interact with
them through likes and comments. It is part of the **fediverse**: you can follow Mastodon or Pixelfed accounts, and be
followed by them, without them needing an account here.

## Joining the instance

Depending on its administrator's choice, you join an instance with an **invitation** (a link received by email or
passed on otherwise), by **open sign-up** (*Créer un compte*), or with an existing **Mastodon account** (*Mastodon*
tab of the sign-in screen, see [With a Mastodon account](mastodon.md)).

A new account can follow, like and comment right away. **Posting** requires the *Publication* permission, granted by
an administrator.

## Signing in

The sign-in screen has two tabs when the instance accepts Mastodon accounts:

- **socialize.example**, for an account created here: your email address or username (`camille` or `@camille`) and your
  password. *Rester connecté* keeps you signed in for 30 days on this device;
- **Mastodon**, for a Mastodon account: your server and your username, without the @ (see
  [With a Mastodon account](mastodon.md#signing-in-with-mastodon)).

The screen reopens the tab last chosen on the device.

## Forgotten password

In the *socialize.example* tab, *Mot de passe oublié ?* asks for the account's email address or username, then emails a
link to choose a new password. The link is valid for one hour and works only once. For a Mastodon account, the
password is changed on its server: *Mot de passe Mastodon oublié ?* leads there.

## Your fediverse address

Your address is `@username@your-instance`. Share it with people on Mastodon or Pixelfed: they type it in their search
to follow you.

## Installing the app

Socialize installs like an app, without any app store:

- on iPhone or iPad, in Safari: *Share › Add to Home Screen*;
- on Android, in Chrome: *Install app* (or *Add to Home screen*);
- on a computer, Chrome and Edge also offer to install it from the address bar.

It then opens full screen and can send [notifications](notifications.md#on-this-device).

## Finding your way

The **menu** (on the left on a computer, at the bottom and in *Menu* on a phone) leads to the feeds, search, the
*Créer* button, notifications, your profile and the settings. The small **?** next to page titles open the part of
this guide that matches the page.

On a wide screen, **Réduire le menu** (at the bottom of the menu) keeps only the icons with their name in small
print; *Agrandir* brings it back, and the device remembers your choice. On a tablet in portrait mode the menu is
always reduced; on an iPad Pro in portrait mode, reducing it shows the suggestions column next to the feed.
