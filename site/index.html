Your photos, your server, your people.

Socialize is a self-hosted photo and video social network with stories, audience groups and Instagram import — federated with Mastodon, Pixelfed and the whole fediverse.

Linux server with Docker · automatic HTTPS · SQLite or MariaDB

Everything you love about photo sharing

…without giving your memories away. Socialize runs on your own server and talks to the open social web.

📸

Posts and carousels

Up to 20 photos and videos per post, alt text, hashtags, mentions, locations and content warnings.

⏱️

Stories

24-hour stories with text and music, highlights on your profile and a private archive.

🌐

Federated

Full ActivityPub support: follow and be followed from Mastodon, Pixelfed and beyond.

👥

Audience groups

Share with everyone, your followers, or only the family and close friends groups you define.

📦

Instagram import

Bring your whole archive — up to 20 GB — with original dates, carousels, EXIF and archived stories.

🖼️

Immich and Synology Photos

Publish straight from your photo library, Live Photos included, with metadata preserved.

🐘

Mastodon sign-in

Friends can use Socialize with their Mastodon account — no new account required.

🔔

Push notifications

Likes, comments, mentions and new posts on phones and desktops, as a home screen app.

🔒

Privacy first

Originals stay private; public copies are stripped of EXIF and GPS. Invitations and permissions for admins.

Up and running in minutes

All you need is a Linux server with Docker and a domain name.

Run the installer

It checks Docker, asks for your domain and generates the secrets.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sadjo/socialize/main/install.sh | sh

Open your instance

Caddy obtains the HTTPS certificate automatically. Log in with the administrator account the installer created.

https://photos.example.org

Invite your people

Send invitations, grant publishing rights, and start sharing.

docker compose pull && docker compose up -d   # upgrade

Prefer Docker Compose, MariaDB or a reverse proxy? Read the installation guide.
