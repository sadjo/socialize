Your photos, your server, your people.

Socialize is a self-hosted photo and video social network, on your NAS or your server, with stories, audience groups and Instagram import — federated with Mastodon, Pixelfed and the whole fediverse.

Why self-host? ▶ Watch the demo
Privacy

Your memories stay at home

On a big platform, your photos keep a company running. At home, they only serve the people you show them to. Follow the journey of a photo.

At home, you decide who sees your photos, and for how long. Nothing is sold.

  • Analysed for advertising
  • Added to your profile
  • Sometimes used to train AI
  • Copied to servers you do not know
  • Encrypted in transit (HTTPS)
  • Kept on your hardware
  • Seen by the people you choose

Your photos are not a product

No ads, no advertising profile, no algorithm deciding what your people get to see.

Your location stays private

Shared copies are stripped of their EXIF and GPS data. The original never leaves your server.

Built into Socialize

You choose who sees what

Everyone, your followers, or only the groups you create: family, close friends.

Audience groups

Nobody can close your album

Your data on your hardware, with free software. And Instagram import to leave without losing anything.

Connected, not centralised

Everyone at home, and still following each other: your instance speaks ActivityPub, like Mastodon and Pixelfed. Your friends can even sign in with their Mastodon account, without creating a new one.

Security at home, with eyes open

Self-hosting protects your privacy, but security is shared: Socialize does part of it, the rest is up to you. Here is the list, with who takes care of what.

  1. Automatic HTTPSThe certificate is obtained and renewed on its own.Socialize does it
  2. Invitation-only accountsYou decide who signs up and who can post.Socialize does it
  3. Private originals, cleaned copiesEXIF and GPS data do not travel with your photos.Socialize does it
  4. Regular backupsIdeally a copy away from home. The documentation explains how.Up to you
  5. UpdatesOne command: docker compose pull.Up to you

Up and running in minutes

All you need is a Linux server with Docker and a domain name.

Run the installer

It checks Docker, asks for your domain and generates the secrets.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sadjo/socialize/main/install.sh | sh

Open your instance

Caddy obtains the HTTPS certificate automatically. Log in with the administrator account the installer created.

https://photos.example.org

Invite your people

Send invitations, grant publishing rights, and start sharing.

docker compose pull && docker compose up -d   # upgrade

Prefer Docker Compose, MariaDB or a reverse proxy? Read the installation guide.
